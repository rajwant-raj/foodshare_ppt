Abstract

At a Glance

Web-Based Platform

A responsive donation portal for restaurants, households & event organizers to list surplus food.

AI Food Detection

Image recognition auto-classifies food type, quantity & freshness for accurate listings.

Smart NGO Matching

Intelligent algorithm matches donations with nearby NGOs based on need, location & capacity.

Analytics Dashboard

Real-time tracking of donations, impact metrics & NGO performance visualization.

3
